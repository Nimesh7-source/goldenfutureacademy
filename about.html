WELCOME TO GOLDEN FUTURE ACADEMY

A place to learn, grow & become.

Golden Future Academy is a smart school located in Lalbandi-9, Sarlahi, Madhesh Province, Nepal. The school brings learning, activities, creativity, technology and opportunities together in one student-focused environment.

WHO WE ARE

Built around the whole student.

Golden Future Academy is presented as a modern learning community where academic learning is supported by practical activities, creativity, technology, sports and student participation.

From the library and science learning to robotics, media, sports, arts, music, dance and social work, the school's wider activities give students spaces to discover their interests and work with others.
